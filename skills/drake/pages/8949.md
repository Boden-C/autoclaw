# 8949

## Entry

- `T/S` is the taxpayer/spouse/joint ownership field. Use `T`, `S`, or `J`; do not use 1099-B reporting category codes in this column.
- Leave `ST` and `City` blank in the CSV unless the source explicitly supports them.
- Populate `S/L` from the source holding-period section: `S` for short-term and `L` for long-term.
- Always populate `1099-B` with `1`, `2`, or `3`.
- Use one combined 8949 form payload unless the user asks to split short-term and long-term.

## Notes

- `1` means basis reported.
- For `1`, summarize within each source document by broker or payer; keep separate rows only as needed to preserve `S/L`.
- Do not summarize `1` across multiple documents or accounts.
- `2` means basis not reported.
- `3` means transaction not reported.
- For `2` and `3`, do not summarize by broker or payer; combine only identical descriptions or asset names.
- Use `8949-DA` for digital assets with 1099-B category codes `4`, `5`, or `6`.
- Use `VAR`, `12-31-YYYY`, or `0` when needed.
