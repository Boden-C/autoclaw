# 8949-DA

## Entry

- Use this page for digital assets only.
- `T/S` is the taxpayer/spouse/joint ownership field. Use `T`, `S`, or `J`; do not use digital asset reporting category codes in this column.
- Leave `ST` and `City` blank in the CSV unless the source explicitly supports them.
- Populate `S/L` from the source holding-period section: `S` for short-term and `L` for long-term.
- Always populate `1099-B` with `4`, `5`, or `6`.
- Prepare `8949-DA` as its own page payload. Do not rely on context from the regular `8949` page.

## Notes

- `4`, `5`, and `6` are digital asset reporting category codes.
- Keep one digital asset payload per requested Drake 8949-DA workflow unless the user asks to split.
- Use `VAR`, `12-31-YYYY`, or `0` when needed.
