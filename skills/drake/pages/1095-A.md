# 1095-A

## Notes

- `Empty 1` through `Empty 10` are intentional empty fields.
- If all 12 months within a column are exactly the same, enter the rounded annual total for that column and leave the monthly entries blank.
- If months differ, calculate the rounded annual total from unrounded monthly amounts.
- When monthly amounts differ, round monthly entries and ensure rounded months add to the rounded annual total.
- If rounded months do not tie to annual total, adjust by $1 starting with the last month that has a number and move backward until tied.
- Apply comparison and rounding separately to enrollment premiums, SLCSP, and advance PTC.
